Community Bulletin

The city council has approved funding for three new pocket parks to be built across the neighborhood over the next year. Construction is expected to begin in early spring, with the first park slated for completion before summer. Residents are encouraged to attend the upcoming planning meeting to share input on amenities such as seating, shade structures, and play equipment.

In addition to the parks initiative, the local library has extended its weekend hours and introduced a new tutoring program for students. Volunteers are still needed to help staff the front desk on Saturday afternoons. Anyone interested can sign up at the front desk or through the community portal.

Finally, the annual food drive kicks off next month, with donation bins placed at the community center, the library, and several local businesses. Non-perishable items are preferred, and organizers are hoping to exceed last year's total by twenty percent.
